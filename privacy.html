Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Overview

Dog Nutrition is a weight and nutrition companion for your dog. We collect the minimum data needed to run the app and never sell your information.

Data we store

Your dog profile, weight and BCS history, food logs, meal templates, body photos, and adaptive review snapshots. This data is stored locally on your device and synced through your private iCloud (CloudKit) so it appears on your other devices. We do not have access to your CloudKit data.

What we do not collect

We do not require an account or login. We do not collect your name, email, or location. We do not use advertising SDKs and do not sell or share data with third parties.

Subscriptions

Pro and Family subscriptions are processed by Apple's App Store via StoreKit. Payment and billing are handled by Apple; we only receive confirmation of your subscription status. Subscription options: Pro Monthly $6.99, Pro Yearly $59.99, Family Yearly $89.99, with a 7-day free trial.

Camera & photos

Camera access is used only to scan food barcodes and read nutrition labels when you choose to. Photo library access is used only when you add body photos or a label photo. Photos you add are stored in your private iCloud and never uploaded to our servers.

Health disclaimer

Dog Nutrition is not a medical device and does not provide veterinary diagnosis or treatment. Calorie targets are estimates based on published nutrition guidelines and your dog's own data. Always consult your veterinarian for medical decisions.

Your choices

You can delete any dog and all of its data at any time from the dog profile screen, which removes it from your device and iCloud. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.
