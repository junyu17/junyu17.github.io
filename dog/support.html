Support

Quick answers for Dog & Cat Nutrition Coach. The app does not require an account or login.

Delete an item or all app data

Open Settings > Manage & delete content. Swipe left on a user-created item to reveal Delete. To remove the in-app profile and all records, use Delete all app data in Settings. This does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open Settings > Subscription > Manage Subscription, or manage subscriptions in your Apple ID settings. Prices and eligible introductory offers are supplied by the App Store for your region.

Restore purchases

Open the in-app subscription screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple ID used for the purchase.

Private iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, app records sync through your private CloudKit database across devices using the same iCloud account. If iCloud is unavailable, the app keeps a local on-device store.

Share feeding with family

Open Settings > Household sharing > Invite family members. Apple's iCloud invitation supports different Apple IDs and shares only feeding coordination events. Each person's full nutrition history and health observations remain private.

Back up or move your data

Open Settings > Export, backup & restore. JSON is the complete restorable backup; CSV is intended for spreadsheets and review.

Nutrition and safety

Dog & Cat Nutrition Coach is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat disease. Profiles with safety conditions remain in logging-only mode until a veterinarian manages the plan.
