How it works

The loop that makes the plan stick.

  1. Choose your pet's species. Enter Dog or Cat, weight, body condition score, activity, and goal. Get a starting daily calorie target based on published nutrition guidelines.
  2. Log each day. Record meals and treats in seconds with templates, barcode, or OCR. The dashboard shows food, treats, and remaining budget.
  3. Weigh in weekly. Add a weigh-in once a week. The engine analyzes the trend — not a single reading.
  4. Adjust with context. Each week the target is gently tuned: keep what works, trim a plateau, or flag a vet visit if something's off.
