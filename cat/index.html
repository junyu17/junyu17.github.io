How it works

The loop that makes the plan stick.

  1. Initial target. Enter your cat's weight, body condition score, activity, and goal. Get a starting daily calorie target based on AAHA / WSAVA nutrition guidelines.
  2. Daily logging. Log meals and treats in seconds with templates, barcode, or OCR. The dashboard shows food, treats, and remaining budget.
  3. Weekly weigh-in. Weigh in once a week. The engine analyzes the trend — not a single reading.
  4. Adaptive adjustment. Each week the target is gently tuned: keep what works, trim a plateau, or flag a vet visit if something's off.
